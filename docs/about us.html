Our Story

Crafting sustainability and passion in every single cup

Rooted in Tradition

Founded in 2000 in Gatineau, Canada, Pura vida Cafe started with a simple belief: good coffee should contribute to the well-being of both the community and the planet. What began as a small roastery has become a haven for coffee lovers who care about environmental responsability.

Ethical Sourcing and Quality

We work directly with organic, fair-trade coffee producers in Costa Rica and Latin America. Each bean is harvested ethically and roasted in small batches locally to ensure intense flavor profiles and sustainable business practices.
