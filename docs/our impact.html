Plant your Own Tree

Every cup of coffee comes with a hands-on reforestation experience.

How the Guided Planting Works

At Pura Vida Cafe, we believe that making an impact should be personal. With every coffee purchase, you will receive a complimentary guided tour of our local nature reserve, where one of our expert environmental guides will help you plant your own native tree.

Your 3 Step Eco Experience

  1. Enjoy Your Coffee: Pick your favorite espresso with our homemade pastries
  2. Meet Your Eco Guide: Join our local guide for a short walk into the surrounding forest trail.
  3. Plant a Sapling: Receive your native sapling, plant it in the soil, and leave a lasting positive mark on nature.
